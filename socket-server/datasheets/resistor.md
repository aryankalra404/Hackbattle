Component: Resistor (220 ohm, standard through-hole)

Notes:
- Resistors are NOT polarized — there is no correct or incorrect 
  orientation/direction for current flow through a resistor.
- 220 ohms is a standard current-limiting value used in series with an LED 
  on a 5V supply to keep current within safe limits (~15-20mA for a 
  typical red LED).
- A missing resistor in an LED circuit (LED wired directly across a 5V 
  supply with no resistor) is a common and serious fault — it can destroy 
  the LED almost immediately due to excessive current.
